# Decision Brief

The audience is an intern who is curious about another line of business but hesitates to reach out for fear that the interest will be read as a transfer request. The artifact should make the lower-commitment path legible: start with curiosity, ask an opted-in employee an information-only question, then decide with firsthand context. The constraint is that F-04 contains only illustrative examples for Business Credit and Treasury Management; this sketch must not imply official role descriptions, live employee data, or a recommendation to transfer. I chose an interactive p5.js sketch because selecting between two example areas makes the exploratory path feel approachable, while a plain guide remains better for precise reading and should be the source of the actual work descriptions.
